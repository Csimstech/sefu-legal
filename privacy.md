# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: May 19, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how SEFU ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the SEFU mobile application ("the App"). By using SEFU, you agree to the practices described here.

## Who We Are

SEFU is a personal growth mobile application that delivers weekly mentor-style challenges across nine life categories. We operate from the United States and serve users primarily in the U.S.

If you have privacy questions, contact us at **support@sefu.app**.

## Information We Collect

We collect only what we need to provide and personalize the service.

### Information you provide directly

When you sign up and use SEFU, we collect:

- **Account information** — your email address (if you sign up with email), or the basic profile details Apple or Google share with us if you use Apple Sign-In or Google Sign-In (name, email).
- **Onboarding answers** — your age band, profession, growth interests, selected focus areas, and notification preferences.
- **Challenge progress** — which challenges you start, complete, or skip, plus your written reflections, action notes, and follow-up notes within those challenges.
- **Journal entries** — any text, mood selections, or titles you save in your personal journal pages.
- **Subscription status** — whether you are on the free tier or have an active premium subscription.

### Information collected automatically

When you use the App, we automatically collect:

- **Usage data** — which screens you visit, which features you use, when you open the App.
- **Device information** — device model, operating system version, and a unique device identifier used by Firebase.
- **Crash and diagnostic data** — if the App crashes, we receive technical logs to help fix bugs.

### Information we do NOT collect

We do not collect:

- Your location (we do not request or use GPS data)
- Your contacts
- Your photos, camera, or microphone
- Your browsing history outside the App
- Sensitive health information beyond what you voluntarily write in your journal

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:

- Create and maintain your account
- Personalize the challenges and content you receive
- Track your progress and streak
- Process subscription purchases through Apple or Google
- Send you notifications you have opted into
- Improve the App by analyzing aggregated usage patterns
- Diagnose and fix bugs
- Respond to your support requests

We do **not** sell your personal information. We do **not** use your data for advertising targeting. We do **not** share your journal entries or reflections with anyone outside SEFU.

## Third-Party Services

SEFU uses the following third-party services to operate. Each has its own privacy policy that governs how they handle data passed to them.

- **Google Firebase** (Authentication, Firestore database, Cloud Functions, Cloud Storage, Analytics) — stores your account data, profile, progress, and journal. See https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
- **Apple Sign-In** (optional sign-in method) — Apple shares your name and email with us if you choose to. See https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
- **Google Sign-In** (optional sign-in method) — Google shares your basic profile info with us. See https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **RevenueCat** (subscription management) — processes subscription status. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

## How We Store and Protect Your Data

Your data is stored on Google Firebase servers in the United States. We use Firebase's standard security rules to ensure only you can access your own data (your account UID is the access key).

We do not store payment information ourselves. All payments are handled by Apple or Google through their respective app stores.

While no system is perfectly secure, we follow industry-standard practices to protect your data.

## Children's Privacy

SEFU is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us at support@sefu.app and we will delete the account.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access** — request a copy of the data we have about you
- **Correct** — update or correct your profile and account information at any time in the App
- **Delete** — request that we delete your account and associated data
- **Opt out** — disable notifications, decline analytics, or stop using the App at any time

To exercise these rights, email **support@sefu.app**. We will respond within 30 days.

### California Residents (CCPA)

If you live in California, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act:

- The right to know what personal information we collect
- The right to request deletion of your personal information
- The right to opt out of the sale of your personal information (note: we do not sell personal information)
- The right to non-discrimination for exercising these rights

To exercise CCPA rights, email **support@sefu.app**.

### European Users (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation:

- Right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection
- Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

Our legal basis for processing your data is your consent (given when you create an account) and our legitimate interest in providing the service you requested.

To exercise GDPR rights, email **support@sefu.app**.

## Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law (for example, tax records related to subscription purchases).

## Notifications

SEFU sends you reminder notifications about your challenges and progress. You can disable these at any time in your device settings or in the App's Settings → Notifications screen.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Effective Date" at the top and notify users via in-app message or email for significant changes. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.

## Contact Us

For any privacy questions or to exercise your rights:

**Email:** support@sefu.app
